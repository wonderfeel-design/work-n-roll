WORK_N_ROLL

Farmers talk. Data gets structured.

A Telegram voice agent that turns farmers' voice notes into CUE-ready field records, linked to the parcels declared in the DUN. Built for the open-source AI hackathon by SA.TECH.

How it works

01SpeakVoice note on Telegram
02ExtractGemma 4 turns it into a record
03ConfirmCard + voice reply in your language
04LearnJudge flags, agronomist corrects

Languages

ENRUCAES → Declaration in Catalan + English

Stack

Gemma 4 E4B · open-weight ElevenLabs TTS + Scribe Telegram Bot API Google Sheets
